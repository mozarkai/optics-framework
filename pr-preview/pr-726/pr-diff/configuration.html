603603
n604 **Purpose:** Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) device automation for non-intrusive mouse/keyboard control.n604 **Purpose:** Drive a phone, tablet, computer or TV that has nothing installed on it. A [airhand](https://github.com/chinmayajha/airhand) dongle in your computer pairs with the device over Bluetooth as a keyboard, mouse and media remote. The driver acts on positions and keys. To find elements, add `camera_screenshot` with the OCR and image strategies.
605
606 **Set up:**
607
608 1. Install the driver: `optics setup --install ble`.
609 2. Install the airhand firmware on an nRF52840 dongle: press its RESET button, then install the firmware zip over USB with `nrfutil`. Pair the device with **airhand**. The [airhand 0.1.2 README](https://github.com/chinmayajha/airhand/tree/v0.1.2#quick-start) gives the steps. On an iPhone or iPad, turn on AssistiveTouch.
610 3. Check the dongle and the link: `optics doctor <project>`.

651 `Launch App` and `Launch Other App` take the name of the app as the device shows it, for example `Settings`. How a device opens an app is a part of its config.yaml, not of the test suite, so one suite runs on phones whose launchers are different. Each method is a sequence of keys and taps:
652
653 | Method | Steps | Default for |
654 |--------|-------|-------------|
655 | `launcher_key` | The launcher shortcut (Cmd+Space, Ctrl+Esc), the name, Enter. On iOS, the driver first presses Home twice, because Home from an app that Spotlight opened goes back to Spotlight. | `ios`, `macos`, `windows` |
656 | `drawer_search` | Home, Meta (the app drawer), the name, Enter. This opens the top result on launchers that filter as you type. | `android` |
657 | `drawer_tap` | Home, Meta, a tap on `app_search`, the name, Up and Enter. Use it when the drawer filters its list only after a tap on its search box. | `android` with `app_search` |
658 | `search_key` | The Search media key, the name, Enter. | — |
659 | `home_icon` | Home, then a tap on the icon above the app's name on the home screen. It needs a camera, and it finds only apps on the first home screen. Some launchers have no app drawer, or give Meta to the assistant. | Last on `ios` and `android` |
660
661 With a camera and an OCR engine, the driver checks each method. After the driver types the name, the screen must show it twice: once in the search box and once as a result. After Enter, both must be gone from where they were. The match ignores case and spaces, joins words that OCR reads apart on one line, and accepts a result label that the launcher cut off with an ellipsis. If a method fails the check, the driver presses Home and tries the next method. When all methods fail, `Launch App` stops with `E0405`. When a method other than the first one opens the app, the log gives the `launch` value that tries it first. Without a camera, the driver uses only the first method that needs no camera, and does not check the result. When the camera reads no text after Enter, the driver does not check the result either, and the log says so.

670 On iOS and Android, `Force Terminate App` opens the app, then closes it from the App Switcher (Recents on Android, opened with Meta+Tab), so that the next `Launch App` starts it from its first screen. The App Switcher can keep a card on a slow frame, so when a test needs the first screen, assert it after `Launch App`. Without a camera, the driver does not check which app opened, so it closes the newest card. `Press Keycode` takes a key name (`enter`, `escape`, `tab`, `f1` to `f24`, the arrows), a combination (`cmd+space`, `ctrl+shift+t`) or a media key (`home`, `back`, `volumeup`, `volumedown`, `mute`, `playpause`, `next`, `previous`, `stop`, `brightnessup`, `brightnessdown`). `search` opens the system search. On a computer, `home` is the Home key.
671
672 The dongle times taps, swipes and typing itself and answers every command. If the device is not connected to the dongle, a keyword waits up to 10 s for it and then stops the run (`E0106`). A swipe or a drag scroll on a touch screen moves at least 0.75 screen heights per second, because iOS takes a slower drag for a tap. On iOS, `Scroll` up or down turns the wheel over the middle of the screen: this moves the content about half a screen, the same distance each time. A drag moves it by a different distance each time, because iOS amplifies a pointer drag. On a computer, `Scroll down` turns the wheel down, which shows lower content only when natural scrolling is off: turn it off for the mouse (macOS turns it on by default). On Android, a `Scroll` drag lasts 4 s, so the content moves about as far as the finger: a faster drag flings the content on, by up to a screen. Element keywords of the driver (`press_element`, `get_text_element`, …) come from the camera instead.
673
674 With a camera, `Press Element` reads one frame and does not wait. After a step that changes the screen, put `Assert Presence` before it: `Assert Presence` waits for the element. On the iOS home screen, tap an app's icon, not its name: use an image of the icon, give `Press Element` on the name an `offset_y` up to the icon, or use `Launch App`.
675
676 On Android, the dongle acts as a touch screen, so taps and swipes are touches with no pointer on the screen. This needs airhand firmware 0.1.2 or later. On iOS and iPadOS, the driver moves the pointer to the top-right corner after each tap and swipe, so that the pointer does not cover the next element in the camera image. This needs airhand firmware 0.1.1 or later.
677
678 **Tips for iOS:** set the hardware keyboard layout to U.S. (Settings > General > Keyboard > Hardware Keyboard), and turn off Auto-Capitalization and Smart Punctuation, so that typed text arrives as sent. Do not press `home` just before `Launch App`: Home a few seconds after an app opened from Spotlight shows Spotlight again, and Cmd+Space then closes it.

680 #### Without a camera
681
682 With `elements_sources: []`, a project drives the device by positions and keys only: `Press By Percentage`, `Press By Coordinates`, `Swipe`, `Scroll`, `Enter Text Using Keyboard`, `Press Keycode`, `Clear Text` and `Launch App`. A keyword that must see the screen, such as `Press Element` or `Assert Presence`, stops with `E0501`. No screenshots are saved.
683
684 #### MCP and `optics serve`
685
686 Start a session with `driver="ble"` and the `platform` and `frame` capabilities. When the server has `WEBCAM_SCREENSHOT_URL` set, the session reads the screen through that camera at the frame size; otherwise it runs without a camera. A remote session names the dongle by `serial`, never by `port`, and reads only the camera the server sets: a `url` on `camera_screenshot` is refused, because the server would fetch any address the caller names.
687

998
999 With `camera_index`, each capture is a new frame: the source discards the frames that the camera kept in its buffer, so a frame after a tap shows the screen after the tap. When the camera opens, the source waits 1 s for the exposure to become stable. Then it locks focus, white balance and exposure if the camera backend lets it. The log gives the capture size, the frame rate and the locked controls.
1000
1001 With `find_screen`, the source finds the screen at the first capture:
1002
1003 | `find_screen` | How the source finds the screen |
1004 |---------------|---------------------------------|
1005 | `brightness` | The driver presses `brightnessdown` 8 times, then `brightnessup` 8 times. Only the screen changes between the two frames, so the source finds the screen on any page that is not black. The screen goes back to its brightness. If it started below the middle level, it ends at the middle level. The `ble` driver has these keys. If the driver cannot press them, or the screen does not change, the source uses `frame`. |
1006 | `frame` | The screen is the bright area in one frame, inside a dark bezel. This needs a bright page from edge to edge, for example Settings in light mode. No driver is necessary. |
1007
1008 The source fits a straight line to each edge of the screen and puts the corners where the lines meet, so rounded corners and a notch do not move them. The screen in the camera must have the shape of `out_width` x `out_height` (4 % tolerance; the source corrects for the camera's angle). The source saves `camera_screen_found_<time>.jpg` in the output folder, with the screen marked, and logs the corners as a `deskew_corners` value. To skip the search, put that value in config.yaml instead of `find_screen`.
1009
1010 Show a bright screen when the run starts. If an edge of the screen is black (dark mode, a black status bar), the source cannot find that edge and stops with `E0305`.
1011
1012 At each capture, the source compares the area around the screen with the same area at the first capture. Screen content cannot change that area. If the phone or the camera moved, the source finds the screen again (`find_screen`) or stops with `E0305` (`deskew_corners`). If the area around the screen has no texture, for example a plain black holder, the source cannot see a movement, and the log says so. With `find_screen` or `deskew_corners`, if the screen shows no lit pixel for 3 s, the source stops with `E0305` and "the screen is off". With `E0305`, the source saves the camera frame as `camera_blind_<time>.jpg`, and `Press Element` and the other element keywords stop at once: no fallback element and no wait can help. `Assert Presence` stops at once too, unless another element source (for example a page source) can find the element: then it continues to look there until its timeout.
