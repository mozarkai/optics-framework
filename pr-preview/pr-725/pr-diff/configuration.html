tt919All engines find text with the same rule. The text must show on the screen as complete words in the same sequence. The words can be on one line, or they can continue on the next line. The rule does not compare letter case or the spaces between words. All other characters must be the same. For example, `Sign in` finds "Sign in to your account", but `Pay` does not find "Payment".
920
921In a script that has no spaces between words, such as Chinese, Japanese or Thai, each character is a word. A tap goes to the center of the words that match. The `index` parameter counts the matches from the top row to the bottom row, and from left to right in a row. To find a text with OCR only, start the locator with `TEXT_ONLY:`.
